01 / THE METHOD

A result is only useful
when its origin is clear.

RealmProof separates a declared expectation from an observed outcome. Source analysis, test execution and verification are distinct operations—and the interface makes those boundaries visible.

The distinction is deliberate. A matching expectation and a simulated result do not prove that an imported contract executed. RealmProof keeps execution provenance and verdicts separate instead of presenting a green checkmark without context.

realmproof

Engineering confidence
through evidence.

RealmProof is an independent verification studio designed to make Gno smart-contract behavior easier to inspect, test and explain. It brings source analysis, scenario design, supported local execution and evidence review into a coherent workflow—without disguising simulations as chain verification.
