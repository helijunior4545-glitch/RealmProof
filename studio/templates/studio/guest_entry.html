realmproof

RealmProof is an independent engineering platform for Gno.land smart contracts. It combines source intelligence, scenario design, supported local Gno execution, security-oriented testing and evidence review. Developers can compare declared expectations with observed behavior, trace results to their source and understand the limits of each verification.

Local tests and browser illustrations are not on-chain proof.
